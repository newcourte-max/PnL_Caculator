Trade P&L Calculator

Contract size defaults to 100,000 units for FX pairs and 100 oz for XAUUSD. Index CFDs (S&P500, Nasdaq 100, Dow, Nikkei 225, DAX 40, FTSE 100) default to $1 per point per 1.0 lot — brokers vary widely here, so adjust "Contract size" to match your broker's contract specs. USDJPY and USDCAD are converted to USD at the current price; AUDCAD uses the USDCAD rate you enter. BTCUSD defaults to 1 BTC per 1.0 lot — adjust "Contract size" if your broker sizes it differently (e.g. mini/micro lots). Spread and swap not included.
